The Mathematics of Football Forecasting

Learn how our analytics engine converts raw metrics into percentage outcomes using standard statistical procedures.

What is the Poisson Distribution?

Developed by French mathematician Siméon Denis Poisson, the **Poisson Distribution** is a discrete probability distribution that expresses the probability of a given number of events occurring in a fixed interval of time or space.

In football analytics, goals can be treated as rare, random events occurring independently throughout 90 minutes. By calculating a team’s expected average goals ($ \lambda $), we can determine the probability of them scoring exactly $k$ goals using the formula:

P(k; λ) = (e-λ * λk) / k!
  • e: Euler's number (approx 2.71828)
  • λ: The Expected Goal value (xG) computed for the match
  • k: The target number of goals (0, 1, 2, 3...)
  • k!: The factorial of k

How does Pro-Strike process variables?

Our predictive algorithm adapts base expected goals through a three-stage adjustment workflow:

1. Baseline Goals

Calculates baseline expectations by taking home attack scores and comparing them to away defense scores, normalized by league scoring standards.

2. Modifying Multipliers

Integrates Home Advantage (+10% Home Att, -10% Away Att), recent form weightings, key squads ratings, and Head-to-Head historical dominance biases.

3. Outcome Aggregations

Constructs an 11x11 probability grid of goal pairings (0-0 up to 10-10) to accurately categorize Home Wins, Draws, and Away Wins.

Why should you trust this predictive model?

While no model can account for red cards, penalty kicks, or extreme tactical shifts on the day, Poisson distribution is the **backbone model used by modern bookmakers and elite predictive models**. By tracking actual team forms and adjusting baseline rates, Pro-Strike provides an objective, math-first assessment that eliminates biased fan emotion.
